WHAT CHANGED — the intensity floor is gone, replaced by the statistic your own round-5 wording pointed to ("zero amplitudes would fit the server's signal exactly"; "removing Graphite changes 428 uploaded samples").
_autoFitGraphiteIsSupported(gPeak, json), from the /api/fit response alone:
    w = 1/max(counts,1)            (the server's weights)
    chi2_with    = sum w (counts - fitted_y)^2
    chi2_without = sum w (counts - fitted_y + y_component)^2      (y_component = individual_peaks[].y of the anchor)
    supported  <=>  amplitude finite and > 0, arrays present and equal length, chi2_without > chi2_with,
                    and ( chi2_with == 0  or  ((chi2_without - chi2_with)/p) / (chi2_with/dof) >= 10 )
    p = the anchor's freely varying parameters, dof = n - statistics.n_free_params.
